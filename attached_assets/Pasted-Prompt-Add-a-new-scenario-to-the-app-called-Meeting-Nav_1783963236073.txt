Prompt

Add a new scenario to the app called "Meeting/Navigating a Partner's Family." Follow the exact same data structure, file conventions, and node format used by the existing scenarios — look at how the current scenario files are structured (question nodes with tag/prompt/options, end nodes with tone/label/scripts/why) and match that pattern exactly. Don't introduce new fields, components, or styling; this should slot in as a new content file the same way the others do.

This scenario is more layered than the others: most branches involve managing both the partner's family AND the partner relationship itself, not just the immediate food situation. Build it with the following structure:

Root question: "Is this the first time meeting them, or an ongoing relationship?"


→ First meeting
→ Ongoing relationship


Branch: First meeting, before food


Question: "Do you want to explain things yourself, or have your partner give them a heads-up first?"
Two end nodes: one for proactively introducing the topic ahead of time, one for handling it live and in-person on arrival.


Branch: At the meal, family serves a homemade/traditional spread


Question: "Does the cook seem confident they made something safe, or are you unsure if they understand cross-contamination?"
End nodes: one script for gently confirming safety without insulting the effort; one for declining a dish you're unsure about while still praising the gesture.


Branch: A family member is skeptical or dismissive


Question: "What are they saying?" with options: "implying you're being dramatic" / "asking if you're just picky" / "comparing it to how things used to be."
Each option leads to its own end node with a script, and the why field should note whether this is a moment to loop the partner in or handle solo.


Branch: Partner doesn't back you up in the moment


This branch is about what to say to your partner afterward, privately — not what to say to the family. The why field should frame the script around preserving the relationship rather than winning the moment.


Branch: Recurring visits — family keeps "forgetting"


Question: "How long has this been happening?" with options distinguishing a one-off slip vs. a repeated pattern.
End nodes: one script for addressing the pattern directly with the family, one for asking your partner to be the messenger instead.


Branch: High-stakes event (holiday, engagement dinner, wedding)


Question: "Do you want to advocate for yourself in the moment, or would you rather eat beforehand and get through the event quietly?"
Write end nodes for both options without implying either is the "better" choice — both are valid strategies.


For every end node, write all three tone variants already used elsewhere in the app (direct/brief/firm) plus a why explanation, matching the voice and length of the existing scenario content.

Add this scenario to the scenario-select list using the same listing format as the others, with a one-line description.
